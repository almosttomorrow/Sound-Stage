What this is actually doing, and why Spotify can't be piped through it

The model. Each room is a real shoebox in metres with real surface materials. The app traces the direct sound and every early reflection off those surfaces with an image-source model, in seven octave bands, then continues the decay with a diffuse tail whose reverberation time comes out of the Eyring equation for that room. Each reflection is placed at both of your ears separately, with the timing and the frequency-dependent shadowing a head of that size would cause. That is what makes the room appear outside your head rather than between your ears. The loudspeaker voicing is applied before the room, in the order reality does it.

The source. Nothing running on a phone can read the audio coming out of another app. On iOS there is no system-audio capture at all, and the streaming services encrypt their playback on every platform. So this app has to be the player. Files work everywhere; on a desktop browser you can also share a tab and process whatever it is playing, as long as that tab is not using DRM.

Headphones, please. The whole point is that you supply the ears. Played on speakers you would be listening to a room inside a room.
